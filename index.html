FAQ

Do you have kids' sizes?

Not right now. Our tees and hoodies come in men's and women's fits only.

Where are products printed?

Orders are printed and shipped by partner print facilities in the United States.

How long does shipping take?

Production and delivery times depend on the product. We'll confirm the timeline with you before you order.

Can I upload my own art or photo?

Yes. Use the Custom Design page to describe your idea and share references, and we'll follow up.

Are doormats, rugs and curtains printed edge to edge?

Yes, they are full-coverage all-over prints.
